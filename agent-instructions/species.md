# species (1)

Corpus assembly for Qualia species records. Find: `synth_find q=species` (or `corpus`, `q42`).

This is the only `qualia.*` catalog morphism. A species or organ is still a `.vibe` that calls locus / manifold / surface ids. Do not add a Rust compiler per species.

## Morphisms

| ID | Args |
|---|---|
| `qualia.species.assemble_corpus` | `corpus` record or `"corpus.q42"` |

Non-fiction biosphere taxa (`domain=biosphere`, `fiction=false`) need evidence `.q42` before `synth_produce_present` (`synth_production_rules`). Encyclopedia sources live in Ecopunk (`C:\github\ecopunk`). `synth_compile_q42` and graph queries stay allowed as the evidence path.
