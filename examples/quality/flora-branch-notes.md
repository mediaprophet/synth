# Ghost gum reference

The recipe uses the existing species-labelled Corymbia aparrerinja canopy and mature flower/gumnut dissection at different scales. These are separate schematic outputs, not a spatially continuous branch-to-flower reconstruction. The procedural canopy, flower proportions, stem junctions and species identity require review against source references.

Native front, side and three-quarter views and both turnaround PNGs were inspected. The flower dissection has visible stamens, buds and a cut interior; the canopy has coarse faceted clumps rather than resolved foliage. These are illustrative baselines, not a reference-approved species likeness or connected branch.
